# Trademarks

"Elsewhere" and "Somewhere", and their logos and app icons (including `resources/AppIcon.png` and
`resources/Logo.png`), are trademarks of Fuzz LLC.

Elsewhere's code is free software under the AGPL, but the licence covers the code, not the names. This page
explains what you can do with the names.

## Fine without asking

- Building Elsewhere from this repository, unchanged, for your own use, under the name Elsewhere.
- Saying that your work is "based on Elsewhere", "a fork of Elsewhere" or "compatible with Elsewhere".
- Writing about Elsewhere, teaching it, and making videos about it.

## Use a different name

If you distribute a changed version of Elsewhere (a fork), give it a different name and icon, and its own
bundle ID (`-DELSEWHERE_BUNDLE_ID=`). That way nobody confuses your version with ours, and bug reports reach
the right people.

## Ask first

Anything else, such as using the names or logos for a product, a company or a domain name. Open an issue or
a discussion to ask.
